Our Services

What We Do

From a single room to a full commercial repaint, West Edge delivers quality painting and decorating services tailored to your property.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trims, and feature walls painted to a smooth, lasting finish. Tidy workmanship and careful preparation every time.

Exterior Painting

Protect and refresh the outside of your property with quality exterior coatings applied properly for lasting durability.

Surface Preparation

Decorating and Wallpaper

Professional painting services for offices, retail spaces, and commercial properties. Minimal disruption, quality finish, and on schedule.

Repaints and Touch-Ups
