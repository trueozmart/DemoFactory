How We Work

Our Simple Four-Step Process

From your first call to the final walkthrough, we keep the process clear and stress-free.

1

Free Quote and Colour Advice

We visit your property, assess the scope and talk through your colour and finish preferences. You get a clear, itemised quote with no hidden costs and no obligation to proceed.

2

Surface Preparation and Priming

We clean, sand, fill and prime every surface before the first coat of paint goes on. Thorough preparation is what separates a finish that lasts from one that chips in a season.

3

Expert Paint Application

We apply premium paint products using the right technique for each surface, whether that is rolling, brushing or spraying, to deliver smooth, even coverage and crisp edges throughout.

4

Walkthrough and Clean-Up

We walk through the finished job with you, address any touch-ups on the spot and leave your property completely clean so you can enjoy the result straight away.
