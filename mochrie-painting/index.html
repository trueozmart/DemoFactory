How We Work

Our Simple Process

From your first call to the final walkthrough, we keep the process clear and hassle-free.

1

Free Quote and Colour Advice

We visit your property, assess the scope and talk through your colour preferences. You receive a clear, itemised quote with no hidden costs.

2

Thorough Preparation

We clean, sand, fill and prime every surface before painting begins. Proper prep is what separates a job that looks good for a season from one that lasts for years.

3

Expert Paint Application

We apply quality paint products using the right technique for each surface, delivering smooth, even coverage and crisp edges throughout.

4

Final Inspection and Handover

We walk through the finished job with you, address any touch-ups and leave your property spotless so you can enjoy the result straight away.
