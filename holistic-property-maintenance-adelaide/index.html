Our Services

What We Do

From routine upkeep to hands-on repairs, we handle the full range of property maintenance tasks so you do not have to.

General Maintenance and Repairs

From leaking taps and broken fixtures to door repairs and general wear-and-tear, we handle the ongoing maintenance that keeps a property functional and safe.

Garden and Landscaping

Regular lawn mowing, hedge trimming, garden clean-ups and general outdoor upkeep to keep the exterior of your property neat, tidy and well-presented year-round.

Cleaning Services

Thorough cleaning for residential and commercial properties, including end-of-lease cleans, routine home cleaning and commercial space maintenance for small businesses.

Minor Renovations

Small renovation and improvement work including tiling, painting, shelving, fencing repairs and other hands-on upgrades that add value and improve the condition of your property.

Handyman Services

General handyman work for all those jobs that need doing around the home or investment property. No job is too small, and everything is completed properly the first time.

Rental Property Care

A reliable maintenance service for landlords and property managers across Adelaide. We help keep rental properties in top condition between tenancies and throughout the year.

1
