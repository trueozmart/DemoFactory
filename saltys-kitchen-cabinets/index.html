Our Services

What We Do

From a full custom kitchen fit-out to a targeted upgrade, Salty's delivers cabinetry solutions built to your brief and your budget.

Custom Cabinet Design

Bespoke cabinet layouts designed around your kitchen's dimensions, storage needs, and aesthetic, from the first sketch to the final plan.

Kitchen Cabinet Installation

Professional installation of your new cabinetry, fitted precisely and finished to a high standard, ready for bench tops and appliances.

Cabinetry Refurbishment

Refresh existing kitchen cabinetry with new doors, drawer fronts, hardware, or finishes. A cost-effective way to transform your kitchen without a full replacement.

Pantry and Storage Solutions

Custom pantry cabinets, pull-out shelving, and integrated storage designed to make every centimetre of your kitchen work harder.

Laundry and Butler's Pantry

Hardware and Finish Upgrades

Update handles, hinges, soft-close mechanisms, and surface finishes to modernise the look and feel of your existing kitchen cabinetry.

How We Work

Simple from Concept to Completion

A kitchen cabinet project should feel exciting, not stressful. Here is how Salty's makes the process clear and straightforward from the first call through to the finished kitchen.

1

Free Consultation

Call Salty's for a no-obligation consultation about your kitchen project. There is no pressure and no commitment, just a conversation about what you are hoping to achieve.

2

Design and Planning

Salty's works with you to design a cabinet layout that suits your kitchen's dimensions, your storage needs, and your aesthetic preferences. Materials, finishes, and hardware are all selected at this stage.

3

Quality Build and Installation

Cabinets are built using quality materials and fitted with precision. Salty's takes the time to get every door, drawer, and shelf aligned correctly so the finished result looks and functions exactly as designed.

4

Final Inspection and Handover

Once installation is complete, Salty's walks you through the finished kitchen and makes any final adjustments. You sign off only when you are completely satisfied with the result.
