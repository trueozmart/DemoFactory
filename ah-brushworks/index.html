What We Do

From new build interiors to full residential repaints, we handle it all with the same attention to detail on every job.

New Build Painting

Complete interior painting packages for new homes, ready for handover. We work with builders and project managers to hit deadlines without cutting corners.

Interior Painting

How We Work

Simple and Straightforward

Getting your home painted should be easy. Here is how we work from the first enquiry to the final finish.

1

Free Quote

Call us or send through some details about the job. We visit the property, measure up and give you a clear quote with no obligation.

2

Colour and Scope Plan

We confirm the colour selections, products and scope of work before we start. No guessing once the brush hits the wall.

3

Professional Application

Proper preparation first, then careful application across every surface. We do not rush coats and we do not skip prep to save time.

4

Walkthrough and Sign-Off

We go through the finished job with you, touch up anything that needs it and leave the site clean. You are happy before we pack up.
