Our Services

What We Do

From full restorations to custom one-off builds, we handle every aspect of automotive customisation in-house.

Custom Modifications

Performance upgrades, suspension work, exterior mods and everything in between, tailored to your exact spec.

Automotive Restoration

Full classic and vintage vehicle restorations, brought back to showroom condition or built to your custom brief.

Custom Paintwork

Single-colour resprays, multi-stage custom finishes, candy coats and graphics, all sprayed in our prep bay.

Panel & Bodywork

Rust repair, panel replacement, dent removal and full body preparation to get the surface right before paint.

Performance Upgrades

Engine, exhaust, braking and drivetrain upgrades to give your vehicle the performance gains you're after.

Detail & Finishing

Final cut and polish, ceramic coating and detailing to protect the finish and make your build look its best.
