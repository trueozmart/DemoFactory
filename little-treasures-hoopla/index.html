How It Works

From First Call to Happy Kids

Planning a children's party with Little Treasures Hoopla is simple, fun, and stress-free.

1

Free Consultation

Call or message to discuss your child's party. We chat about dates, themes, guest numbers, and ideas, and we'll give you a clear outline of what's possible. No obligation.

2

Design and Planning

We build the party concept around your child's interests and your vision. Themes, décor, activities, and timing are all confirmed so everything is locked in ahead of the big day.

3

We Bring the Magic

On the day, we set up, style, and run the entertainment so you can focus on your child and your guests. Vibrant, joyful, and carefully executed from start to finish.

4

Happy Kids, Happy Families

We wrap up the celebration making sure every child leaves with a smile. We pack down and leave the space tidy, and you're left with a day full of happy memories.
