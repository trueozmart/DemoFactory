Why Adelaide Heritage Painting

The Right Painters for Your Heritage Home

Period homes deserve a painter who understands them. We bring the skill and care that heritage properties require.

Correct Prep for Period Surfaces

Heritage surfaces need a different approach. We assess every substrate, repair cracks and prime properly before any paint goes on, protecting the character of your home for years to come.

Punctual, Tidy and Professional

We show up when we say we will, work cleanly around your home and leave the site spotless each day. You will always know where the job is up to and when it will be finished.

Deep Adelaide Heritage Knowledge

Adelaide's character homes are built from limestone, sandstone, bluestone and heritage-era timbers. We understand these materials and know how to treat them correctly so the result lasts.

Our Services

What We Do

From full exterior repaints to careful interior finishing, we handle the full range of painting for period and character homes.

Heritage Home Painting

Full interior and exterior painting for period homes, with heritage-appropriate colour palettes and materials that respect the original character.

Stone and Render Restoration

Specialist painting and repair of limestone, sandstone and rendered heritage surfaces, restoring them to their original appearance and protecting them from the elements.

Interior Painting

Complete interior painting including walls, ceilings, cornices, skirtings and architraves, finished to a standard that matches the craftsmanship your home deserves.

Exterior Painting

Full exterior repaints for character homes including weatherboard, brick, stone and render, properly prepared to withstand Adelaide's climate.

Timber and Trim Work

Careful preparation and painting of heritage timber windows, doors, soffits and fascias, preserving the original joinery that gives your home its character.

Colour Consultation

Heritage-appropriate colour guidance to help you choose palettes that suit your home, match the era of the property and sit well in the local streetscape.

How We Work

Simple Process, Exceptional Results

Getting your heritage home painted should be straightforward. Here is how we make it easy from the first call to the final walkthrough.

1

Free Quote

Call us or send some photos. We visit the property, assess the surfaces and provide a clear, detailed quote with no obligation and no pressure.

2

Colour and Preparation Plan

We work through the colour palette, surface preparation requirements and sequence of work with you before anything begins. No surprises once we start.

3

Expert Prep and Paint

Every surface is filled, sanded and primed correctly before paint goes on. We take the time that heritage homes need and never skip steps to save time.

4

Walkthrough and Clean-Up

We walk you through the finished result and make sure you are completely happy before we pack up. The site is left clean and tidy every time.
