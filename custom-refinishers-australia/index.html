Our Services

What We Do

From full resprays to intricate custom artwork, we handle the full range of automotive and surface refinishing work.

Automotive Respray

Full vehicle resprays in any colour, from stock OEM finishes to custom metallics, pearls and two-tone combinations.

Custom Paint and Airbrushing

Hand-painted custom designs and airbrushed artwork for truly one-of-a-kind results on cars, bikes and panels.

Panel Refinishing

Expert repair and refinishing of damaged or worn panels back to factory standard or better, with colour-matched blending.

Motorcycle Painting

Custom paint and refinishing for motorcycles including tanks, fairings and chrome work, to any spec you have in mind.

Surface Preparation

Full strip-back, blocking, sanding and priming to give any coating the solid foundation it needs to last and look perfect.

Chrome and Detailing

Precision chrome finishing and final detailing to complement any custom paint job and complete the look of the build.

How We Work

From Quote to Completion

A clear, no-fuss process so you always know where your vehicle is at and what to expect.

1

Free Quote

Bring your vehicle in or send us photos. We assess the job and give you a clear, detailed quote with no obligation and no pressure.

2

Colour and Finish Plan

We help you nail down the right colour, finish and any custom design elements before any work begins. Stock, custom mix, metallic, pearl or artwork.

3

Expert Prep and Application

Full strip-back, prime and apply. Every coat is checked before the next goes on. No shortcuts, no rushing. Just quality done properly.

4

Final Inspection and Delivery

We walk through the finished result with you before handover. We don't call a job done until you're completely happy with it.
