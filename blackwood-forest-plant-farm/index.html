Why Blackwood Forest Plant Farm

Native Plants Grown for Local Conditions

From single garden beds to full revegetation projects, we supply healthy, regionally appropriate plant stock backed by real local knowledge.

Healthy, Regionally Grown Stock

Every plant is propagated and grown on site at Dalyston to suit local soils and conditions, giving you stronger establishment and better survival rates than generic nursery stock.

Straightforward Advice, No Guesswork

Whether you're a home gardener or managing a large revegetation project, we take the time to talk through what will actually thrive on your site before you buy.

Deep Roots in the Gippsland Region

Based in Dalyston, we understand the coastal winds, clay soils and rainfall patterns across the Bass Coast, and select species that are genuinely suited to this environment.

What We Offer

Native plant supply, propagation and revegetation support for landowners, landscapers and home gardeners across the region.

Native Plant Supply

A wide range of native Australian plants and shrubs ready for garden or property planting.

Forestry Seedlings

Revegetation Consultation

Land Restoration Planting

Plant Propagation

Home Garden Natives

Native varieties selected to suit home gardens across the Bass Coast and Gippsland.
