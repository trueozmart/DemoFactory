FAQ

Gold Coast Plumbing and Gas Questions

Quick answers to what Gold Coast homeowners ask us most.

What areas of the Gold Coast do you service?

We service the whole Gold Coast, from Coolangatta in the south to Logan in the north and out to Tamborine in the hinterland. That includes Burleigh Heads, Robina, Southport, Nerang, Helensvale, Coomera, Pimpama, Ormeau and Beenleigh.

Are you a licensed plumber and gas fitter?

Yes. We are QLD licensed for plumbing, drainage and gas fitting, and fully insured for every job. We work in Queensland only.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Call 0422 453 929 and we will talk through the job and give you a clear, upfront price with no obligation.

Do you install and repair hot water systems?

Yes. We install, replace and repair electric, gas continuous flow, storage and heat pump hot water systems, including element and thermostat replacements.

Can you help with an emergency like a burst pipe?

Yes. Call us straight away for burst pipes, leaking taps and water service failures. We will get out to you as fast as we can.

Do you handle plumbing for bathroom renovations?

Yes. We do the full plumbing side of bathroom renovations, from drainage and water rough-in through to fitting basins, toilets, baths and tapware.
