Crema Marfil Ivory is designed to capture the timeless elegance of natural marble. A refined porcelain tile that is crafted on a brilliant super white body, that showcases crisp veining and sophisticated detail. Available in two striking colours; Bianco and Ivory and two finishes; Matt and Silk.

The Silk surface is a luxury porcelain tile, with 3D shaped technology, that has a natural marble look with a smooth, silky, honed finish. Silk surfaces are meticulously engineered to engage the visual and tactile senses, delivering a captivating sensory experience.
