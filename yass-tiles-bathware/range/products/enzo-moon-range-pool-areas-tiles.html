Product Description:Enzo Moon is a collection of porcelain floor and wall tiles, designed to offer a natural modern stone effect for indoor and outdoor use.  With its natural colour variations and meandering veins, it allows you to create environments with a modern and minimalism approach. With a range of four colours; Moon, Sand, Cinder and Coal, Enzo will remain perfectly on trend for years to come.  Tiles can be used both indoor and outdoor surfaces, allowing you to have the one tile. Available Mosaics: Hexagon, Chevron and Subway. Available Accessories: Enzo Crazy Pave sold as a set of A and B patterns together. Sizes:  2.16m2 total in a box, sold as a complete set.
