Rhythm is a stone-look porcelain tile that features a soft matt finish that enhances its natural character with understated elegance.  Subtle veining and delicate tonal shifts flow across the tile surface, capturing the organic depth of real stone that is both versatile and timeless. It offers the authentic character of stone, ideal for creating serene sophisticated spaces. Available in 4 colours; White, Desert, Silver and Coke.

Available Accessories:  Brick Decor Feature Tile and Round Edge Bullnose (White and Desert only).
