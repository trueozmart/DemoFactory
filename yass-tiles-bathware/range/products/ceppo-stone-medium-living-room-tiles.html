Product Description  Ceppo Stone Medium is a porcelain tile that emulates a Terrazzo stone look on your surfaces.  The design features large aggregate stone chips that work together, creating a texture of natural Ceppo stone. Available in Light, Dark and Medium colours. The Ceppo Stone range has a Textured finish. Available Mosaic: Picket.
