FAQ

In-Home Care in Mudgee: Common Questions

What areas do you service?

Kiran's Kare Service supports clients in Hargraves, Mudgee, Gulgong, Hill End, Windeyer and surrounding areas of the Mid-Western Region.

What services do you offer?

Personal care, domestic assistance, transport to appointments, community and social access, in-home respite and support after a hospital stay.

Who do you support?

Older Australians who want to stay living at home, people living with disability, and anyone needing short-term help at home after hospital or surgery.

Are you checked and insured?

Yes. Kiran holds an NDIS Worker Screening Check, Working with Children Check and National Police Check, plus public liability and indemnity insurance.

Will I see the same carer every visit?

Yes. Support is one-to-one, so the same familiar face arrives every visit.

How do I get started?

Call 0488 508 077 for a free, no obligation chat about the help you or your loved one needs. We then meet at home and plan your support together.
