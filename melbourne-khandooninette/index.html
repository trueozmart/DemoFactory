Our Services

Painting and Renovation Services for Melbourne Homes

Interior and exterior painting plus the finishing trades that make your renovation complete.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trims, and doors finished to a professional standard. Drop sheets down, surfaces protected, and the space left spotless when the job is done.

Exterior Painting

Full exterior repaints using quality paints suited to Melbourne's climate, protecting your home from the elements while dramatically improving street appeal.

Rendering

Professional rendering for exterior walls, delivering a clean, durable finish that transforms the look of your home and provides lasting protection.

Tile Work

Tiling for bathrooms, kitchens, and living areas. Precise layout, quality adhesion, and a neat finish that complements the paintwork throughout the home.

Plumbing Touch-Ups

Minor plumbing repairs and touch-ups as part of a renovation job, so you do not need to coordinate multiple trades for small fixes alongside your painting project.

Repaints and Touch-Ups

Refreshing tired paintwork, fixing patchy areas, and matching existing colours accurately so the finished result looks consistent throughout the whole property.
