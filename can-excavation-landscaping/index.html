FAQ

Excavation and Landscaping in the Eurobodalla: Common Questions

What areas do you service?

We are based in Bodalla on the NSW South Coast and service Narooma, Moruya, Batemans Bay and the wider Eurobodalla Shire.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed landscapers (Licence No. 399085C) and fully insured.

Can your excavator get into a tight backyard?

Yes. Our 2 tonne tight access excavator fits through a 990mm opening, so small backyards are no problem.

What auger sizes do you have?

We have augers in 100mm, 300mm and 450mm, plus a 450mm rock auger, along with a tipper for moving soil and material.

Do you work on farms and rural blocks?

Yes. We look after homes, farms and rural blocks right across the Eurobodalla, from small backyards to big rural jobs.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free with no obligation. We assess the site and give you a clear price.
