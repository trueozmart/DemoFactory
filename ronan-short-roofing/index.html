Our Services

Complete Roofing Solutions for Brisbane Properties

Whether you need a repair, a full replacement, or a routine inspection, Ronan Short Roofing has it covered.

Roof Repairs

Cracked tiles, missing sheets, ridge repointing, valley repairs and flashing replacement. Leaks tracked down and fixed properly, not just patched over.

New Roof Installations

Metal and tile roof installations for new builds and full replacements. Correctly installed from the substrate up, with the right materials for Queensland's climate.

Roof Inspections

Thorough roof inspections that identify issues before they become costly problems. Honest, detailed reports with clear recommendations and no unnecessary upselling.

Roof Maintenance

Preventative maintenance that extends your roof's life: cleaning, repointing, re-bedding ridge caps, and addressing minor wear before it becomes major damage.

Gutter Replacement and Repair

New gutter installations, repairs to damaged sections, and downpipe work. Properly pitched and sealed to handle Brisbane's intense rainfall without overflow or pooling.

Leak Detection and Fixing

Expert diagnosis of roof leaks, including hard-to-find sources around skylights, flashings, and penetrations. Fixed properly so the problem doesn't return next rain season.

How It Works

From First Call to Finished Roof

1

Free Quote

Call to describe the job. We'll arrange a roof inspection and provide a clear, written quote covering what needs doing and why. No obligation and no pressure.

2
