Our Services

Fencing and Decking for Every Melbourne Home

Custom solutions that improve security, privacy, and outdoor appeal across residential properties.

Timber Fencing

Custom timber fencing installed to suit your property's style and privacy needs, with hardwood and treated pine options that look great and hold up over time.

Colorbond Fencing

Durable Colorbond steel fencing in a range of colours, ideal for properties needing a low-maintenance, long-lasting boundary solution with a clean, modern look.

Deck Construction

New timber and composite decks designed and built to extend your living space outdoors. Structurally sound, beautifully finished, and built to meet Australian standards.

Deck Restoration

Worn or weathered decks restored to their best condition through sanding, recoating, board replacement, and structural repairs that bring them back to life.

Fence Repairs

Storm damage, rotting timber, leaning posts and broken sections repaired properly, not just patched. Fences that look right and function correctly after every repair.

Outdoor Renovations

Broader outdoor renovation work combining fencing, decking, and structural improvements to transform underused yard spaces into functional, appealing outdoor areas.
